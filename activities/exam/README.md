# Fall 2026 - Advanced Operating Systems Midterm Exam Instructions

## 1. Contributing a Problem (Optional)

You may optionally contribute **one problem** to help shape the midterm exam.

- **One problem per student:** Submit one problem in any format you choose.
- **File format:** Submit your problem as a `.txt` or `.md` file.
- **Images:** If your problem includes an image, upload it separately as a `.png` or `.jpg` file.
- **Submission location:** Upload your files to **Submitty**.
- **Deadline:** Submit your problem by **Sunday (10/18/2026) at 12:00 p.m. (noon)**.

Participation is optional. You may take the exam whether or not you submit a problem.

## 2. How Submitted Problems Will Be Used

I will create exam questions based on the problems students submit. The version that appears on the exam may differ substantially from the original submission, but I will aim to test the **same underlying knowledge and concepts**.

For example:

- If your submitted problem tests knowledge of **stack security**, the exam version will also test stack security.
- If your submitted problem tests knowledge of **lottery scheduling**, the exam version will also test lottery scheduling, though it may approach the topic from a different angle.

The modified version may be **much more difficult or much easier** than the submitted problem. Both are possible: a straightforward submitted problem may become a challenging exam question, while a particularly challenging submission may become a much easier exam question.

If the number of submitted problems is too large to include all of them, I may select only a subset for the exam. **Every exam question will be based on student-submitted problems**, but not every submitted problem is guaranteed to appear on the exam.

On the other hand, if the number of submitted problems is insufficient, I will create additional problems myself. These instructor-created problems may make the exam less predictable. Therefore, if you prefer a more predictable exam, you are encouraged to submit a problem.

## 3. What Kinds of Problems Should You Submit?

Submitted problems should be **closely relevant to the materials covered in Advanced Operating Systems**.

However, some problems on topics we have not emphasized in class are also welcome if they are useful for your learning and professional development. For example, you may submit problems about the use of the C `static` or `volatile` keywords. Although we have not emphasized these keywords in class, they frequently appear in technical job interviews, so practicing questions about them may be beneficial.

## 4. Compiled Collection of Submitted Problems

After the submission deadline, I will compile the submitted problems into a single PDF and post it on the **course calendar by Sunday (10/18/2026) at 5:00 p.m.**

Use this PDF to review the topics represented by the submitted problems.

## 5. Exam Content and Weighting

**100% of the exam questions will be based on student-submitted problems.** However, the exam versions may differ significantly from the original submissions in wording, format, difficulty, scenario, or the specific task required.

Focus on understanding the underlying concepts rather than memorizing the submitted problems or their solutions.

## 6. Exam Materials and Prohibited Devices

- You may bring **crib sheets** to the exam, with **no limit on the number of pages**.
- **Electronic devices, including watches and smartwatches, are not permitted.**
